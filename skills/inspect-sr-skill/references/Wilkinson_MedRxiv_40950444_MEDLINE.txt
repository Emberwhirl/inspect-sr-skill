TI  - INSPECT-SR: a tool for assessing trustworthiness of randomised controlled trials.
LID - 2025.09.03.25334905 [pii]
LID - 10.1101/2025.09.03.25334905 [doi]
AB  - The integrity of evidence synthesis is threatened by problematic randomised 
      controlled trials (RCTs). These are RCTs where there are serious concerns about 
      the trustworthiness of the data or findings. This could be due to research 
      misconduct, including fraud, or due to honest critical errors. If these RCTs are 
      not detected, they may be inadvertently included in systematic reviews and 
      guidelines, potentially distorting their results. To address this problem, the 
      INSPECT-SR (INveStigating ProblEmatic Clinical Trials in Systematic Reviews) tool 
      has been developed to assess the trustworthiness of RCTs. This will allow 
      problematic RCTs to be identified and excluded from systematic reviews. This 
      paper describes the development of INSPECT-SR. The tool and an associated 
      guidance document are presented.
